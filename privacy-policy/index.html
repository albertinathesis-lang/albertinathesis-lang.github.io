Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This page explains what happens to your personal data when you visit this website or write to us. The short version: the site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising, and contacts no third party while you read it. The only personal data we ever hold is what you choose to send us.

Who we are

AI Art Lab is a public interest company based in Luxembourg, working where art meets artificial intelligence. AI Art Lab is the data controller for this website.

Registered address and register number: to be added before publication.

Questions about this policy, or about your data, go to director@nftorino.com.

What this site does, and does not do

  • It is a static website. There are no accounts, no logins and no user profiles.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no local storage to identify you.
  • It runs no analytics, no advertising and no social media trackers.
  • Every file the page loads (scripts, fonts, images, the opening scene) comes from this site's own address. No third-party server is contacted while you browse.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. To deliver a page, GitHub's servers receive the technical data any web request carries: your IP address, the address of the page requested, the time, and your browser's identification string. GitHub may keep this data in its server logs for security purposes. We do not receive it, and we have no access to it. How GitHub handles it is described in GitHub's privacy statement. GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States; its transfers of data from the European Union are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Staying in touch

The site has a box where you can leave your email address to hear from the lab. Sending it hands the address to us by email. We use it for one purpose: to contact you about the lab's work. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by writing to director@nftorino.com; we then delete the address. We do not pass it to anyone else, and we do not use it for anything else.

Writing to us

If you email us, we keep the exchange for as long as it takes to deal with it, and afterwards only as long as we have a legal reason to. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, or the steps needed before entering into an agreement with you.

Links to other sites

The site links to other websites, for example the LinkedIn and ResearchGate profiles of the people on the team, and the site of the studio that designed it. Once you follow a link you are on that site, under its own privacy policy. The browser is told not to pass on the full address of the page you came from.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask us at any time for:

  • access to the personal data we hold about you, and a copy of it;
  • correction of data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • deletion of your data;
  • restriction of how we use it, or an objection to our using it;
  • a copy of the data you gave us in a machine-readable form;
  • the withdrawal of any consent you gave, with effect for the future.

Write to director@nftorino.com. You also have the right to complain to the Luxembourg supervisory authority, the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), cnpd.public.lu.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If the site starts doing something new with data, this page changes first, and the date at the top with it.
